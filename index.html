How StudentHub works

Approve the organisation, add student records, then unlock student features.

1

Organisation requests access

A representative requests a workspace and the global admin approves it.

2

Admin adds student data

Organisation admins add student names, college emails, roll numbers, courses, and years.

3

Students login and collaborate

Students login with their college email and use only the student features for that organisation.

Colleges join

How colleges join and manage feedback

A college, department, or student organisation requests a workspace. After approval, its organisation admin can upload verified student data, manage features, and understand what students need.

01

Request workspace

Organisation representatives request access and explain who the workspace is for.

02

Admin approval

The global admin approves the request and promotes the requester as organisation admin.

03

Upload students

The organisation adds student names, college emails, roll numbers, courses, and year.
