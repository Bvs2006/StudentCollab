Roadmap

Stacks and queues

Linked lists

DFS, BFS, height, LCA, traversal patterns, validation.

BFS, DFS, topological sort, shortest path, DSU basics.

1D DP, 2D DP, knapsack, LIS, memoization to tabulation.

08

Mixed timed sets, explain approach, complexity, edge cases.

Pair practice

How two students should solve together

  1. One student explains the brute-force idea.
  2. The other student asks about constraints and edge cases.
  3. Both choose the pattern before coding.
  4. One codes while the other dry-runs examples.
  5. Swap roles on the next problem.
Interview prep

Weekly mock routine

  • Monday to Thursday: 3 topic problems per day.
  • Friday: one 45-minute timed medium problem.
  • Saturday: revise failed problems and notes.
  • Sunday: mock interview with a friend or teammate.
